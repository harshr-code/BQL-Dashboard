BQL Requirement Simulator

How many BQL each channel needs to keep a city's SCs at full meeting capacity, at the BQL → MS and MS → MD rates you set. Actual BQL for the chosen city and month is filled in from the live dashboard data.

How it works. MD capacity = active SCs × MD per SC per day × working days in the month. Each channel is assigned a share of that capacity; its BQL needed = MD needed ÷ MS→MD ÷ BQL→MS. Gap = BQL available − BQL needed (positive means surplus).
BQL available is actual BQL for the month; for the current month it is projected to a full month from the days reported so far.
Defaults. Conversion rates start at a PAN placeholder (BQL→MS 35%, MS→MD 62%) until city × channel MS and MD data is loaded. Capacity shares start at each channel's current share of BQL. SC count is entered manually until the manpower sheet is connected. All inputs are editable and are remembered in this browser.
